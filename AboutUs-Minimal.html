01 / Story

Started as two engineers. Still answers its own email.

Scriptiqo was founded in Pune in 2019 by two engineers who had spent a decade at agencies and couldn't find one that still cared about the craft the way they did.

Seven years later we are a team of twenty — still small by design, still senior by default. We work with a short list of clients at a time, from early-stage founders to public companies, across fintech, healthcare, logistics, and climate.

In 2023 we opened a second practice: applied AI. It turned out the same principles that make a good platform make a good agent — clear constraints, tight feedback loops, and someone who stays in the room after launch.

03 / Values

Four positions we argue from.

V01

Small, senior teams.

Three to five practitioners per engagement, each with five or more years of shipping work. No staff augmentation, no hidden juniors.

V02

Write it from scratch.

We'd rather spend a week on a good abstraction than a month working around a bad one. We start with the problem, not a stack.

V03

Estimates we can defend.

The timeline and budget you see in week one is the one we sign to. We price in the uncertainty instead of pretending it isn't there.

V04

Stay after launch.

Every engagement ends in an operate phase — measuring, maintaining, improving. The fun part starts once real users arrive.

04 / Process

Four phases, no surprises.

Phase 01
Discover
Two weeks reading your docs, interviewing your team, and mapping constraints. We write the brief, not you.
Phase 02
Shape
A single document — scope, risks, milestones, exit criteria. Both sides sign. The plan is the contract.
Phase 03
Build
Two-week iterations. Working software at the end of each. You see the product, not a PowerPoint of the product.
Phase 04
Operate
Post-launch retainer: observability, incident response, monthly improvements. We stay on until you ask us not to.
